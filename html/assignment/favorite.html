How to Fry Egg

  1. Heat the pan: Place a non-stick skillet on the stove over medium heat.
  2. Add 1 teaspoon of butter or cooking oil and swirl to coat the bottom of the pan evenly.
  3. Break the egg gently into the warm pan, making sure not to break the yellow center.
  4. Sprinkle a small pinch of salt and black pepper directly onto the egg.
  5. Let it cook for 3 to 4 minutes until the white part turns solid white and is no longer clear.
