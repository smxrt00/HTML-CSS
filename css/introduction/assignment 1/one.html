Ingredient

  • Yam: 1 medium tuber of white African yam (peeled and sliced)
  • Water: Enough to cover the yam slices in the pot.
  • Salt: 1 teaspoon (or to taste) for savory flavor.
  • Vegetable Oil or Palm Oil: 1–2 tablespoons (optional, drizzled or used for an accompanying dip/sauce).
  • 1 small onion or ground dry pepper (optional, for serving with a quick side sauce).

Preparation

  1. Cut and PeelCut the yam tuber into thick round slices.Use a sharp knife to slice away the rough outer brown skin.Cut the peeled yam into smaller, even chunks or cubes.
  2. Rinse and CleanPlace the cut yam into a bowl of clean water.Wash the pieces thoroughly to remove dirt and excess surface starch.Rinse until the water is clear and not cloudy.
  3. Transfer to PotPut the cleaned yam pieces into a large saucepan or pot.Pour in enough fresh water to completely cover the yam.Leave some space at the top of the pot because yam can foam and boil over.
  4. Season and CookAdd a teaspoon of salt to the water.Place the pot on medium heat and cover with a lid.Cook for 15 to 20 minutes until the pieces become soft
  5. Test and DrainPoke the yam with a fork or a knife to check for softness.Drain the hot water immediately so the yam does not become soggy.Serve the cooked yam hot with your favorite sauce.
